Privacy Policy

Noesis Health  |  Athena Core Technologies, Inc.  |  Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: April 27, 2026
HIPAA Notice: Noesis Health is designed for use by healthcare providers and insurance organizations as a covered entity business tool. We operate as a Business Associate under HIPAA where applicable. All Protected Health Information (PHI) is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and in transit (TLS 1.3+).

1. Who We Are

Noesis Health is operated by Athena Core Technologies, Inc. ("Athena Core", "we", "us", "our"), a healthcare revenue cycle management (RCM) technology company. Our platform connects healthcare providers with insurance companies to facilitate claims submission, eligibility verification, prior authorization, and payment workflows.

Contact: privacy@noesishealth.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Name, email address, and password (hashed, never stored in plaintext)
  • Organization name and National Provider Identifier (NPI)
  • Professional role (provider staff, practice administrator, insurance representative)
  • Billing information (processed by Stripe — we do not store full card numbers)

2.2 Clinical and Administrative Data (PHI)

In the course of providing our services, we process Protected Health Information on behalf of covered entities, including:

  • Patient names and dates of birth (stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM)
  • Insurance member IDs and payer information
  • Diagnosis codes (ICD-10-CM) and procedure codes (CPT/HCPCS)
  • Prior authorization requests and clinical notes
  • Claims data, explanation of benefits (EOB), and remittance information

This information is processed solely for the purpose of providing RCM services and is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising purposes.

2.3 Usage and Technical Data

  • IP address, device type, browser type, and operating system
  • Session timestamps and feature usage (for platform improvement)
  • API request logs (retained for 7 years per HIPAA audit requirements)
  • Audit trail of all PHI access events

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide claims processing, eligibility verification, and prior authorization services
  • To authenticate users and maintain session security
  • To generate denial prevention analysis and reimbursement estimates
  • To fulfill HIPAA audit and compliance requirements
  • To send service notifications and billing communications
  • To improve platform features and fix technical issues
Important Disclaimer: Noesis Health is a workflow and administrative tool. It does not provide medical, legal, tax, or insurance advice. All reimbursement estimates are reference values based on CMS published data and are not guarantees of payment. Coverage, authorization, and payment determinations are made exclusively by the applicable payer, provider, or administrator. No output from this platform should be construed as a medical recommendation or clinical decision.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Payers and clearinghouses: Claims data is transmitted to insurance payers and EDI clearinghouses as directed by your organization in the normal course of claims processing.
  • Service providers: We use Stripe (payment processing), PostgreSQL cloud hosting providers, and Redis cloud providers who act as processors under appropriate data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect our legal rights.
  • Business associates: We enter into HIPAA Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all vendors who process PHI.

5. Data Retention

  • PHI and audit logs: 7 years minimum from date of service (HIPAA §164.530(j))
  • Account data: Retained for the duration of the subscription and 3 years thereafter
  • Session data: 30-day automatic session expiration (HIPAA §164.312(a)(2)(iii))
  • Billing records: 7 years (IRS and financial compliance requirements)

6. Security

  • All PHI encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM with organization-specific keys
  • All data transmitted over TLS 1.3 or higher
  • JWT-based authentication with token blacklisting on logout
  • Automatic session termination after 30 minutes of inactivity (HIPAA requirement)
  • Complete audit trail of all PHI access events, retained 7 years
  • PHI paths sanitized in audit logs to prevent incidental PHI exposure
  • No PHI written to analytics or error tracking systems

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your account data (subject to HIPAA retention requirements)
  • Export your organization's claim and administrative data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@noesishealth.com.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use session cookies for authentication purposes only. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party analytics trackers that share data with advertising networks. We do not participate in behavioral advertising.

9. Children's Privacy

Noesis Health is designed for use by licensed healthcare professionals and insurance representatives. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, and the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your CCPA rights, contact privacy@noesishealth.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will notify active users of material changes to this policy via email at least 30 days before the change takes effect. Continued use of the platform after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

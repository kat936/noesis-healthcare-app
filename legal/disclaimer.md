## Strategy Engine Disclaimer

### What the Strategy Engine IS

The Strategy Engine is a **deterministic rules-based system** that scores healthcare claims based on predefined rules about:
- CPT-to-diagnosis code compatibility
- Medical necessity indicators
- Timely filing requirements
- Duplicate claim detection
- Coding modifiers
- Bundling and unbundling rules

### What the Strategy Engine IS NOT

The Strategy Engine is **NOT**:
- An artificial intelligence system
- A machine learning model
- An insurance company adjudication system
- A guarantee of claim approval or denial
- An expert claims analyst or certified coder
- A substitute for professional claims management
- A clinical decision-making system
- A prediction tool with guaranteed accuracy

### Strategy Engine Limitations

**Scores are analytical / informational decision-support only.**

- Scores do NOT mean a payer will approve or deny a claim
- Scores do NOT represent actual claim adjudication
- Scores do NOT account for individual payer contracts, policies, or local medical review policies
- Scores are based on generic healthcare rules, not your specific payer agreements
- Scores should NEVER be the sole basis for any healthcare claims decision
- Claim denial estimates are NOT guaranteed outcomes
- Approval rate predictions are NOT actual performance metrics
- The platform makes NO warranty about claim success rates

**You remain responsible for:**
- Independent claims validation
- Compliance with coding standards
- Verification of claim data accuracy
- Communication with payers about specific policies
- Professional claims management

## NPI Registry Data Disclaimer

### Data Accuracy

The National Provider Index (NPI Registry) data displayed in this platform:
- Comes directly from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)
- May contain outdated information
- May contain incomplete or inaccurate entries
- Reflects CMS records at the time of query
- Should be independently verified before use

### Your Responsibility

Before relying on NPI data:
- Independently verify all provider credentials
- Confirm current licensing status with state licensing boards
- Verify active participation with payers
- Confirm current contact information and practice locations
- Check for any practice restrictions or sanctions

**Do NOT rely solely on NPI Registry data for critical healthcare decisions.**

## OpenFDA Data Disclaimer

### Drug and Device Information

OpenFDA drug and device data displayed in this platform:
- Is sourced from FDA public databases
- May not reflect the most current drug labeling or device information
- Is informational only and not approved medical guidance
- Should not be used as the sole source for prescribing or clinical decisions
- May contain incomplete adverse event reporting

### Limitations

- FDA databases do not contain all possible adverse events or interactions
- Reporting to FDA is voluntary and may not reflect true incidence rates
- Drug interactions, contraindications, and warnings may be incomplete
- Device safety and effectiveness data may lag current clinical experience

### Your Responsibility

**Consult authoritative sources for clinical decision-making:**
- Current FDA-approved prescribing information
- Micromedex or other drug interaction databases
- Current clinical literature and guidelines
- Licensed pharmacists or prescribers
- FDA MedWatch program for adverse events

## Logo

### Primary Logo

The Noesis.io logo consists of a geometric mark (concentric circles, equilateral triangle, and compass star pattern) above the wordmark "NOESIS.IO" rendered in gold on a dark background.

### Logo Versions

| Version | Mark Color | Background | Use Case |
|---------|-----------|------------|----------|
| Primary | Gold (#C5A55A) | Black (#000000) | Default for all dark contexts |
| App Dark | Gold (#C5A55A) | Deep Navy (#0F172A) | In-app header and sidebar |
| Reverse | Deep Navy (#0F172A) | White (#FFFFFF) | Light background print/web |
| Monochrome | White (#FFFFFF) | Any dark background | Single-color applications |

### Clear Space

Minimum clear space around the logo equals the cap height of the letter "N" in the wordmark on all four sides. No other graphic elements, text, or edges may intrude into this space.

### Minimum Size

- **Digital:** 24px height minimum (geometric mark + wordmark)
- **Print:** 0.5 inches (12.7mm) minimum height

### Prohibited Uses

- Do not stretch, distort, skew, or rotate the logo
- Do not change logo colors outside approved versions listed above
- Do not add effects (drop shadows, glows, outlines, gradients, 3D) to the logo
- Do not place on busy, patterned, or low-contrast backgrounds
- Do not recreate or approximate the logo --- use only official files
- Do not separate the geometric mark from the wordmark in formal/public contexts
- Do not animate the logo without written approval
- Do not use the logo to imply endorsement of third-party products

## Typography

### Digital (App and Web)

- **Headings:** System UI sans-serif stack (`system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`)
- **Body:** Same system sans-serif stack
- **Monospace:** System monospace (`ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, 'SF Mono', Menlo, Consolas, monospace`) for claim IDs, CPT codes, NPI numbers

### Print and Presentations

- **Headings:** Arial Black
- **Body:** Arial
- **Formal Documents:** Georgia (serif) for cover pages and executive materials

### Type Scale

| Element | Size | Weight | Context |
|---------|------|--------|---------|
| Page Title | 28-32px | Bold | Module headers |
| Section Header | 20-24px | Semibold | Card titles, section breaks |
| Body Text | 14-16px | Regular | Primary content |
| Caption / Label | 12px | Medium | Table headers, badges, metadata |
| Small / Legal | 11px | Regular | Footer text, legal notices |

## Voice and Tone

### Brand Voice

- **Professional:** Institutional tone appropriate for healthcare administration
- **Clear:** No unexplained jargon; no vague or aspirational language
- **Protective:** Conservative about stated capabilities; accurate about limitations
- **Trustworthy:** Healthcare context demands precision over persuasion

### Language Rules

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Rules-based claim validation" | "AI-powered claim validation" |
| "Analytical tools" | "Decision support system" |
| "Informational estimates" | "Predictions" or "guarantees" |
| "Healthcare administration platform" | "Healthcare intelligence platform" |
| "Sample data / demo data" | Presenting mock data as real metrics |
| "Strategy Engine (deterministic rules)" | "Machine learning model" |

### Required Disclaimers in Marketing

Any public-facing material referencing platform outputs must include one of:

- "Not medical advice. Not a substitute for licensed professionals."
- "Informational tool only. Users remain responsible for all clinical and billing decisions."
- "Powered by Athena Core Technologies."

## Legal Marks

All public-facing materials must include:

### Copyright Notice

> (c) 2026 Athena Core Technologies. All rights reserved.

### Trademark Symbol Usage

- Use (TM) after "Noesis.io" and "Noesis.io Health" until federal registration is granted
- Use (R) only after USPTO registration is confirmed
- First mention in any document should carry the symbol; subsequent mentions may omit
